# Hospital Family Communication UX Design

UX/HCI project designing a hospital communication application to keep families and caregivers informed and connected to a loved one's care.

## Project Overview

This project was completed as a UX/HCI design project focused on improving communication between hospitals, patients, and family members or caregivers who are unable to be physically present during a hospital stay.

The project was inspired by a personal experience involving a family member who required emergency hospital care. The uncertainty and lack of immediate information during the experience became the basis for exploring how technology could help family members feel more informed, connected, and involved in a loved one's healthcare.

Using an extreme persona and scenario, the project examines the emotional and informational needs of users during a stressful healthcare experience. The experience was analyzed and then translated into requirements for a proposed digital application.

The resulting design vision combines patient-location tracking, barcode technology, notifications, healthcare information, instant messaging, and video communication to help authorized family members and caregivers remain connected with patients and healthcare providers when they cannot be at the hospital.

## Project Goals

- Understand the emotional and informational needs of family members during a loved one's hospital stay
- Use persona and scenario development to explore the user experience
- Analyze a real-world experience to identify opportunities for technology-based solutions
- Develop high-level functional and usability requirements
- Provide understandable information about a patient's care and treatment
- Improve communication between family members and healthcare providers
- Allow authorized users to receive updates about a patient's location and care
- Design an experience that promotes feelings of safety, comfort, control, and relief
- Create a concept that is straightforward and usable during a time-sensitive situation

## Design Process

### Empathize

The project began by examining a personal experience involving a family medical emergency and the difficulty of receiving information when unable to be physically present at the hospital.

A fictional extreme persona and scenario were developed to explore the experience from the perspective of a parent separated from a child receiving emergency medical care.

### Understand

The experience was broken down and analyzed using the four threads of experience:

- Sensual
- Emotional
- Compositional
- Spatial-temporal

The project also examined six processes of sense making:

- Anticipating
- Connecting
- Interpreting
- Reflecting
- Approaching
- Recounting

This analysis helped identify important moments involving uncertainty, communication, safety, control, and access to information.

### Ideate

The insights from the experience analysis were used to develop a digital application concept designed to connect authorized family members and caregivers with a patient's hospital experience.

Proposed features included:

- Patient-location tracking
- Barcode-based patient movement tracking
- Access to lab and scan information
- Clear explanations of healthcare information
- Text and video communication with healthcare providers
- Requests for additional information
- Notifications about important changes or emergencies
- Secure access for authorized family members and caregivers
- Simple and straightforward navigation

## Design Vision

The proposed application was designed around two primary objectives.

The first objective was education by providing family members and caregivers with clear, understandable information about a loved one's healthcare.

The second objective was to create feelings of comfort and security by helping users remain informed and connected when they could not physically be present at the hospital.

The design emphasizes simplicity because the application would be used during stressful and time-sensitive situations where users should not need extensive training to understand how to use it.
